# 🎓 Smart Kaksha

> **AI-powered classroom learning platform that turns lectures into personalized learning experiences.**

Smart Kaksha is an AI-powered EdTech platform designed to bridge the gap between traditional classroom teaching and personalized learning. Teachers can upload recordings of their lectures, and AI automatically converts them into structured notes, identifies important concepts, and generates assessments for students.

The platform also analyzes student performance to identify weak topics and provide **personalized revision recommendations**.

## 🚀 How It Works

```text
Teacher Conducts Class
        ↓
Upload Class Recording
        ↓
AI Transcription
        ↓
Topic & Concept Detection
        ↓
AI-Generated Notes
        ↓
AI-Generated MCQs
        ↓
Student Takes Assessment
        ↓
Performance Analysis
        ↓
Weak Topic Identification
        ↓
Personalized Revision
```

## ✨ Features

- 🎙️ **AI Lecture Transcription** — Convert classroom recordings into text.
- 📝 **Automatic Notes Generation** — Generate concise and structured notes from lectures.
- 🧠 **Topic Detection** — Identify important topics and concepts covered in each class.
- ❓ **AI-Generated Assessments** — Automatically generate MCQs based on lecture content.
- 📊 **Performance Analysis** — Analyze student responses and identify knowledge gaps.
- 🎯 **Personalized Revision** — Recommend revision based on individual weak topics.
- 📚 **Missed-Class Support** — Students who are absent can access notes from the lecture.
- 👨‍🏫 **Teacher Review** — Teachers can review and verify AI-generated learning material.

## 🎯 Problem We Solve

Traditional classrooms generally provide the same learning experience to every student, even though students have different learning speeds, knowledge gaps, and levels of understanding.

Smart Kaksha creates a continuous learning cycle where classroom content is converted into learning resources and student performance is used to determine what each student should revise next.

## 💡 Core Idea

Instead of treating every student the same:

**Teach → Capture → Understand → Assess → Analyze → Personalize**

Smart Kaksha turns a single classroom lecture into an adaptive learning resource for every student.
